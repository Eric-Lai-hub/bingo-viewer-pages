隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 11 日 / Last updated: 11 September 2026

中文

缤果看图（英文名 Bingo Viewer）不收集任何个人信息。

  • 完全在你的 Mac 本地运行，不会发起任何网络请求。
  • 读取的图片仅用于在本机显示，不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 不会修改你的原始文件。导出与格式转换会在你指定的目录生成新文件；移到废纸篓可在应用内撤销。
  • 不含统计、广告或任何第三方 SDK，因此没有数据可被收集或共享。
  • 不要求注册或登录，不索取任何账号信息。
  • 只请求你自己选择的文件夹的访问权限，授权保存在本机，可随时在系统设置中撤销。

English

Bingo Viewer does not collect any personal information.

  • Runs entirely on your Mac and makes no network requests.
  • Images are read only to display them locally and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Original files are never modified. Export and conversion write new files into a folder you choose; moving to the Trash can be undone in the app.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs — there is no data to collect or share.
  • No account is required and no personal details are asked for.
  • Access is requested only for folders you pick; that permission is stored locally and can be revoked in System Settings at any time.
